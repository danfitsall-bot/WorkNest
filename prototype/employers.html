Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes. Cancel any time from your dashboard — no calls required. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How is WorkNest different from Indeed or LinkedIn?

WorkNest is a niche board — every candidate is actively seeking flexible, parent-friendly work. You get fewer applications, but much higher quality and intent. Our average application-to-hire rate is 3.2x higher than generic job boards.

What does "Parent Friendly" verification actually involve?

We review your flexible working policy, parental leave policy, and conduct a brief survey with 3–5 of your employees. Verification typically takes 5 business days. Companies are re-verified annually.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — all Starter and Growth plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You can post jobs and see applications before committing.

Can I advertise non-flexible or office-based roles?

We only list roles with at least one genuine flexibility feature (remote, part-time, flexible hours, compressed hours, etc.). This protects the trust of our candidate community and ensures your listings reach the right audience.
