About the role

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join Figma's product team. You'll work across core product surfaces, shaping the experience for millions of designers worldwide. This role is designed for someone who does their best thinking when they're not clock-watching.

What flexibility actually looks like here

This isn't "flexible working on request". Here's what we mean:

  • 4-day week is contractual — Fridays off, every week, no questions asked
  • Core hours are 10am–3pm — outside that, work when you're most effective
  • All meetings are recorded and transcribed for async review
  • School pickups, assemblies, sports days — just block your calendar
  • Childcare emergency? Slack your manager, sort your family, no admin required

What you'll be doing

  • Own end-to-end design for 1–2 major product areas
  • Collaborate with PMs and engineers from discovery through to shipping
  • Run user research sessions and synthesise findings into design decisions
  • Mentor junior designers and contribute to our design system
  • Present work to senior stakeholders and the wider team

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years in product design, with strong portfolio of shipped work
  • Deep expertise in Figma (naturally)
  • Experience running user research independently
  • Strong written communication — you'll document your thinking clearly
  • Career returners: we actively encourage applications from parents returning after a break

Benefits

  • £65,000–£80,000 salary depending on experience
  • 4-day working week (contractual)
  • Private healthcare (includes family cover)
  • £2,000/yr childcare support budget
  • £1,000 home office setup budget
  • 25 days holiday + bank holidays
  • Enhanced parental leave — 26 weeks full pay for primary caregiver
  • EMI share options
